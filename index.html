Stuart, Florida

Where We Fish

Home port of Stuart, Florida — We sit at the heart of the Treasure Coast, offering some of the most diverse inshore fishing on Florida's entire East Coast. The Indian River Lagoon, St. Lucie River, and pristine nearshore wrecks and reefs create an unmatched fishery right outside our back door.

At BlackJack Fish Co., we specialize in light tackle action but we can tailor every trip for the experience you are after.

What We Target

Prime Nearshore Targets

The Treasure Coast waters hold some of the hardest-fighting, most sought-after inshore species in Florida. We chase them all with light tackle and an intimate knowledge of their behavior.

Snook
The apex inshore predator. Ambush hunters in the mangroves, bridges, and passes — your heart will pound every single cast.
Redfish
Bulldogs from the mangroves — aggressive feeders with powerful runs, copper color and black spots you won't forget.
Trout
Speckled sea trout roam the Indian River grass flats year-round. Patient, technical, and genuinely fun on light gear.
Permit
Wary, fast, and picky — landing one is a milestone that separates the anglers from the fishermen.
Jacks
Jack crevalle are pound-for-pound among the strongest fish in the ocean. A bruising fight on light tackle that never gets old.
Tarpon
Nicknamed the Silver King. One of the most thrilling fish in the ocean — explosive jumps, screaming runs, and sheer power that will test every angler to their limit.

Meet Your Guide

Captain Jason Stelmacki

Captain Jason Stelmacki has spent years learning his craft both offshore and inshore. Based out of Stuart, Florida, Jason grew up with a fishing rod in his hand and developed an obsession with the technical challenge of the nearshore hunt.

His approach is simple: work hard to put clients on fish and teach them about the local environment. Every trip is a hands-on lesson leaving you a more knowledgeable angler.

It does not matter if it's your first time holding a spinning rod, Jason will put together a trip that challenges and rewards you. The goal is a quality experience that will live in your memory for years.
